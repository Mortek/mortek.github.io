Light Through Jelly: Making Gummy Icons Glow Like Candy

What happens when you hold a gummy bear up to the window, and then draw that for 3,000 apps.

By Maurice Moret · November 2026 · 3 min read

Hold a gummy bear up to a window and the light glows straight through it. That small moment is the whole idea behind Gummy. Its icon is a little game controller moulded from jelly, which says most of what the pack is about: soft, squishy and a bit silly.

Flavours, not colours

The symbols look moulded from see-through jelly, with shiny thin edges, window reflections and tiny air bubbles trapped inside. We call the ten colours flavours: blueberry, bubblegum, lemon, lime, cherry, grape, lagoon, liquorice, tangerine and mint. The tiles are squishy jelly squares, plain, swirled or sprinkled with sugar dots.

Making jelly with code

Gummy is rendered by our own tools from the same set of generic glyphs the other packs use. What changes is the material. The renderer lets light pass through the shape, bends it a little, and puts bright spots along the edge. The air bubbles are placed per icon, so no two jellies look identical. The tricky balance is transparency: too clear and the symbol disappears, too dense and it looks like plastic, so we tune it until a row of icons reads like a bag of sweets.

What is inside

  • Almost 3,000 icons in one style, in ten flavours: blueberry, bubblegum, lemon, lime, cherry, grape, lagoon, liquorice, tangerine and mint.
  • A themed tile for apps that have no icon of their own yet, in this case a jelly tile, plain, swirled or with sugar dots, so the grid has no gaps.
  • An app that follows your phone into light or dark mode, with search across the whole set.
  • An icon request screen. Tap Send and the list of apps you see, plus an email if you type one, goes straight to the developer. That is the only time the app goes online.
  • No ads, no analytics and no subscription. It is a one-time purchase of EUR 1.99 on Google Play.

All the symbols are generic. They are drawn by our own tools, and none of them copies a company logo, so no app is promised an exact match. Apps get a symbol that suits what they do, in a colour close to their own.

How to use it

  1. Install a launcher that supports icon packs. Many do, and your current one may already.
  2. Open Gummy and go to the Apply tab. Pick your launcher for a one-tap apply, or open the launcher settings and choose Gummy as the icon pack.
  3. Later, after the pack has been updated, tap Refresh icons in the app to apply the new set again. If the launcher still shows old icons, restart it.
