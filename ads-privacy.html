Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who this applies to

This policy covers a personal tool operated by Maurice Moret, a sole proprietor and author. The tool connects to the Amazon Ads API to manage advertising campaigns for my own books.

What data is used

The tool accesses only the Amazon Ads account of its owner. It reads and updates campaign settings, keywords, bids, budgets and performance reports. It does not collect, store or process personal data about any other person, and it has no other users.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or disclosed to third parties. Credentials are kept private by the account owner.

Retention

Campaign and performance data stays in the owner's Amazon Ads account and in local files on the owner's computer. It can be deleted at any time.
