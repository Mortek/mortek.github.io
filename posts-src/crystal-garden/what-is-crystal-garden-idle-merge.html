I wanted to make a game that feels like watering a small garden: you plant something, you check in for a minute, and when you come back there is a little more of it. Crystal Garden: Idle Merge is the result, a free Android game where gems grow into crystals on floating islands. It is out now on Google Play, and this is the story of what it is and why it works the way it does.

The idea in one sentence

You plant gem seeds, drag two equal gems together, and they become one better gem. Every gem earns gold by the second, so the more beautiful your garden gets, the faster the gold rolls in, even while the game is closed.

18 floating islands, 108 gems

The game is built around 18 floating islands. Each one has its own sky, scenery and mood, from Sunset Meadow to Sakura Gardens and beyond. Every island has six tiers of gems, which makes 108 gems in total, and each one has its own shape and colour. You unlock the next island when you have earned enough, and you can hop between them on the map, because all your islands earn at the same time.

Merging on a 5 x 5 board

Each island has a 5 x 5 merge board. You buy seeds, drag equal gems together, or tap two of them to merge. It is the same loop that makes merge games easy to pick up, with one rule I added to keep it gentle: there is no clock chasing you. If you like the idea of an idle game that plays itself but want to do something with your hands for a minute, merging is a nice middle ground.

Shadow blight, and a way to turn it off

To keep the board from being too static, shadow blight creeps onto empty cells and slows the income next to it. You tap it three times to cleanse it, and you earn a gem for doing so. Not everyone wants that, so there is a Calm garden setting that turns blight off completely. It is your garden, so you decide how busy it is.

It keeps earning

The core of any idle game is what happens when you are not looking. In Crystal Garden every gem earns gold per second, and your islands keep earning offline. When you open the game again, a Welcome back screen shows the gold that came in, and you can watch an optional ad if you want to double it. There is also a tap button that gives a burst of income: tap it in a rush, then let it recover while you merge. For more on how idle income works in general, see idle base and offline earnings explained.

Goals and daily rewards

Every island has goals, and there is a long list of achievements. They pay out gems when you claim them. On top of that there is a daily reward with a seven day streak, and I made it forgiving on purpose: miss one day and the streak stays, miss two and it starts over.

The gem shop

Gems are rare, and that is the point. You earn them by cleansing blight, finishing goals, claiming daily rewards and unlocking islands. You then spend them in the gem shop on upgrades such as cheaper seeds, faster tap recovery, longer offline earnings and a x2 income boost. You can also change how your garden looks, with Dusk, Night and Aurora lighting and gold, rainbow and star sparkle looks.

How it makes money, honestly

Crystal Garden is free to play. Ads are optional rewarded ads only: you choose to watch one for a bonus, and nothing pops up in your way. If you would rather skip the ads, Remove Ads turns every ad bonus into an instant free claim. Gem packs exist if you want to speed up, but nothing is needed to finish the game. There is no account and no sign-up, and you can reach me from Settings with the built in support chat. Read the privacy policy and terms if you want the details.

Soft, quiet and in your language

The game has soft music, gentle sounds and optional vibration. It speaks 12 languages: English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.

Who it is for

If you like merge games, collection, soft visuals and games you can open for a minute without feeling behind, this is for you.
