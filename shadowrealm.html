Shadowrealm: Age of Darkness

A dark fantasy role-playing game by Mortek. Build your character, conquer cities, forge gear, and rise through the ranks against rivals from across the realm.

About the Game

Shadowrealm: Age of Darkness is a free-to-play browser-based RPG developed and operated by Mortek (Maurice Moret). Players create a character and progress through a multi-city dark fantasy world — completing actions, crafting equipment, trading on the marketplace, competing in PvP and arena combat, joining guilds, and climbing the ranks.

The game is played entirely in a web browser and requires no download or installation. An account is required to play. The core game is free; optional purchases of Shadowgems are available for players who wish to accelerate their progress or acquire cosmetic items.

Features

Character Progression

Rank up your character, allocate stats, and unlock new actions across four cities.

Crafting & Gear

Gather materials, forge equipment, and equip your character with items across 12 slots and multiple rarities.

PvP & Arena

Challenge other players in PvP battles and compete in a rated arena league with an ELO ranking system.

Marketplace & Economy

List gear and materials on the player-driven marketplace; trade Shadowgems via the escrow system.

Guilds & Social

Create or join a guild with up to 20 members, pin announcements, and coordinate with your team.

Quests & Challenges

Complete daily and weekly quests for rewards and track your achievements over time.
