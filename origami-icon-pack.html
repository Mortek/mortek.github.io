About this icon pack

Fold your home screen. Origami remakes your launcher as paper craft: a sheet of coloured paper, folded once or twice, with the light catching every edge. About 2,900 icons are ready.

THE FACETS

Each symbol is cut from coloured paper and creased into flat facets. One facet faces the light, the next turns away, and a fine crease marks the line between them. Paper fibre shows in the colour, and a pale edge runs round every sheet so even the darkest paper keeps its outline.

THE LAYERS

Parts of a symbol are separate sheets laid on top of each other, each throwing a soft shadow on the one below. The result is shallow and clean, like a card you could hold: a camera with a paper lens, an envelope with a folded flap, a pin standing up from its shadow.

THE SQUARE

Behind every symbol lies a square sheet with its own folds: both diagonals, a cross fold or a single valley fold. Ten paper colours cover the range from indigo and sakura to matcha, persimmon and saffron. The symbol for a busy app is picked by its job, and the paper by its usual colour. A leftover app still gets a plain folded tile, as long as your launcher lets a pack supply a blank one.

FOLD IT ON

Your launcher must support icon packs. Open Origami, tap Apply, and the whole screen refolds in one go. The dashboard has search, follows your light or dark setting, and has a Request screen where Send asks us for any missing app.

HONEST NOTE

We drew each symbol ourselves and kept it generic. Not one resembles a corporate emblem, and no app publisher took any part. Ads and tracking are absent; the app connects to the internet solely when you tap Send. A single payment, no subscription. Made by Mortek.
